🏛️ Tamil Culture

Tamil culture is rich in traditions, festivals, food, music, dance, literature, and art. It reflects the deep connection between Tamil people, their language, nature, and community.

Pongal is one of the most important Tamil festivals. It is a harvest festival that celebrates nature, farmers, cattle, and family togetherness.

Tamil traditional arts include Bharatanatyam, Karagattam, Villupattu, and various folk dances. These art forms help preserve Tamil heritage and pass it from one generation to the next.

Tamil cuisine is also an important part of the culture, with traditional foods such as rice, sambar, rasam, idli, dosa, and many regional dishes.
